What to Expect From Your First Laundry Pickup & Delivery

Laundry pickup and delivery is built to be simple, and your first order only takes a few minutes to set up.

1. Schedule your pickup

Use our Need a Delivery or Pickup? link or give us a call, pick a time that suits you and let us know any specific instructions.

2. Leave your bag out

Put your laundry in a bag and leave it where the driver can reach it. You do not need to be home.

3. We wash, dry and fold

Your laundry is weighed, washed to your preferences, dried and neatly folded.

4. Delivered back to your door

Clean clothes come back packed and ready to put away. You will get a notification when your order is on its way.
